# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss the work without attacking the person. Welcome questions and different levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. They will consider context and aim for a proportionate response. Participants may ask for clarification or reconsideration respectfully.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. For a project-specific concern, contact the maintainer through an available private channel; do not publish sensitive details in an issue. Retaliation against people who raise concerns in good faith is not acceptable.
